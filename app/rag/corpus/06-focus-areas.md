# Share of work by focus area

- AI development, around 30 percent: conversational AI, semantic search, summarization, image analysis.
- Web development, around 25 percent: web apps, dashboards, marketing sites.
- Custom software, around 20 percent: bespoke platforms unifying fragmented workflows.
- Mobile apps, around 15 percent: native-feeling iOS and Android experiences.
- Low-code and no-code, around 10 percent: rapid builds on Bubble, FlutterFlow and Framer.
