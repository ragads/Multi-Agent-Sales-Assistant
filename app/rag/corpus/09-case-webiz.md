# Webiz - the office, on demand (Israel, launched July 2026)

Webiz lets professionals and companies book premium offices and meeting rooms by the hour with no
long-term contract. The software reaches past the screen and into the building itself.

# The problem

Traditional office rentals are rigid, expensive and a poor fit for how people work now. Companies pay
for space that sits empty and juggle access, scheduling and billing across systems that do not talk to
each other.

# What CloseFuture built

A complete ecosystem - a mobile app, an admin dashboard and an on-site tablet application - on a
backend that unifies discovery, booking, payments and device control:

- QR-code instant booking: on-site tablets show codes for immediate access and real-time availability.
- Flexible scheduling: hourly, future-dated, extended and permanent bookings.
- Company wallet: a shared team credit pool with member management.
- Dynamic pricing: bonus credit packages, per-order discounts and auto-renewal.
- Secure payments integrated through Rivhit.
- IoT control: automated doors, electricity and air-conditioning, all from the app.

# Outcome

Launched July 2026 in Israel, turning rigid office rentals into flexible, self-service access and
automating what used to be manual building management.
