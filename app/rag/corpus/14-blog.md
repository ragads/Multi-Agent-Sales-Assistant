# Building scalable apps with FlutterFlow (21 May 2026)

A guide to architecting FlutterFlow apps that grow from MVP to thousands of users. The core argument is
that scalability is a shared job between a strong backend (Supabase, Firebase or custom APIs doing the
heavy lifting) and a disciplined frontend. It covers backend-first architecture, clean relational data
models, pagination and lazy loading, consistent naming, careful state management, reusable components
and row-level security.

# Neon vs Supabase: a 2026 choosing and migration guide (29 April 2026)

A comparison of two Postgres platforms - Neon, a serverless database with autoscaling and branching,
versus Supabase, a full backend bundling auth, storage, realtime and auto-generated APIs. The rule of
thumb it lands on: Neon for composable, bursty, ephemeral setups; Supabase for fast MVPs and standard
SaaS. Migrating between them is easy in theory but one to three weeks of real work.

# FlutterFlow for Enterprise (21 May 2026)

Examines FlutterFlow's viability for enterprise deployments across scalability, performance,
third-party integrations and security, arguing that with the right backend and architecture, low-code
is a legitimate choice well beyond the MVP stage.
