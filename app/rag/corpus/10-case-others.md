# Randevmeste - speed dating platform (2025)

A platform for offline speed-dating events where guests meet up to 30 matches a night in short rounds.
The client had outgrown a Wix-only setup with no guest tracking, real-time communication, filtering or
notifications. CloseFuture built a custom Bubble app integrated with Wix over API, so tickets bought on
Wix flow straight into the database. Guests register by ticket number, build profiles and move through
event phases with real-time liking and matching, instant match alerts, secure post-event messaging,
safety tools and host controls. The client, Jozef Greftak, said the app exceeded their expectations in
both design and functionality.

# Vigo - women's fitness app (2025)

A fitness app built specifically for women, with personalized workouts, meal tracking and AI nutrition
analysis, because generic fitness apps miss the mark on personalization and motivation. CloseFuture
built goal-based onboarding and plans, real-time activity and calorie tracking that adjusts
dynamically, custom diet plans with portion guidance, AI meal analysis through a WhatsApp bot where you
log a meal with a photo, and subscriptions via RevenueCat. Launched 2025 in Israel with strong
retention.

# Galaxy Move - Samsung creator community (2025)

A mobile-first community platform connecting Samsung creators and fans through gamified engagement,
built because existing tools lacked interactivity and scale. CloseFuture built a gamified
task-and-points system, community posting with direct admin contact, a real-time leaderboard,
role-based admin controls separating super admins from limited community managers, and reward
redemption for gifts and coupons. Delivered as mobile and web apps.
