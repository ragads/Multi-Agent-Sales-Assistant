# The four stages

1. Discovery - clarify goals, scope features, choose the right tools and set a roadmap before anything
   is built.
2. Product design - turn requirements into responsive, polished designs, refined together until they
   are right.
3. Development - rapid, tested builds using low-code and AI, with regular updates so nothing is a
   surprise.
4. Maintenance - ongoing support for performance, upgrades and growth after launch.

# Delivery promise

Most projects go from idea to launch in four to six weeks. The operating model behind that promise is
keeping the team small and senior and standardizing on a handful of platforms it knows deeply, which
lets CloseFuture quote in days and deliver in weeks.
