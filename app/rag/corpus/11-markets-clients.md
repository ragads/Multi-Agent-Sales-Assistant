# Where the work is

CloseFuture's shipped work clusters in a few markets. A strong run of projects sits in Israel - Dipy,
Webiz and Vigo all launched there - alongside enterprise work for a global brand (Samsung, via Galaxy
Move) and European clients such as the team behind Randevmeste. The published writing points at the
Gulf (Dubai, Riyadh) and Europe (Germany, France, Spain, Poland) as active markets, with India as the
home base.

# Typical clients

- Early-stage founders validating an idea
- Funded startups needing a polished MVP fast
- Enterprises wanting AI features shipped quickly
- Established brands building community or internal tools

# Problem shapes they suit

- Fragmented, multi-tool workflows to unify
- Manual processes ready for automation
- Marketplaces with two-sided complexity
- Products needing AI woven into the core flow
