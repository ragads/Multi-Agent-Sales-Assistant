# Baskaran Manimohan, founder

CloseFuture is led by Baskaran Manimohan, who also runs the personal brand Baskaran Builds and is a
familiar name in the low-code and AI development community. He stays close to delivery: in client
reviews, Baskaran is the contact named for responsiveness, hands-on involvement and clear
communication.

# Background

His path runs through the heart of the no-code movement, with time in and around Bubble, Era of No Code
and GrowthX, sharpened by an IIT Patna generative-AI certification and study at EDHEC Business School.
He often mentions growing up in a small village in India, which shapes the studio's guiding idea: that
high-quality product development should be fast and affordable enough for anyone with a good idea.
