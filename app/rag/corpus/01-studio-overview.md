# Who CloseFuture is

CloseFuture is an AI product studio that turns ideas into production-ready web and mobile
applications, usually in four to six weeks rather than four to six months. It was founded in 2023 and
runs out of Velur, Namakkal, Tamil Nadu, India.

The studio is small and senior. It pairs low-code platforms such as Bubble, FlutterFlow and Framer with
AI-assisted development and a solid backend, so software ships in weeks and still holds up in
production with real users, real payments and real scale.

# Headline numbers

- 4-6 weeks from idea to launch on most projects
- 5.0 client rating across verified reviews
- 100+ shipped products
- Founded 2023

# Who comes to them

Two kinds of clients. Founders come to validate an idea quickly and get a polished MVP in front of real
users on a lean budget. Enterprises come for secure, scalable tools and AI features they need faster
than an in-house roadmap allows. In both cases the studio behaves less like a vendor and more like an
extension of the client's own team.

# What they believe

Speed with substance - fast delivery that still survives production. Transparency - honest, continuous
communication. User-centric design - products shaped around real needs. Partnership - acting as part of
the client's team. Global support - coverage across time zones. Problem-solving - finding a way rather
than saying no. A verified client review puts it as: they never say "not possible", they figure it out.
