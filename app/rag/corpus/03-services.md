# What CloseFuture does

End-to-end product development, from the first whiteboard sketch to long-term care.

# Web and mobile apps

Full builds - web apps, mobile apps, dashboards, tablet and kiosk interfaces - on low-code foundations
with custom backends where needed.

# Product design and UX

Design is treated as a specialty, not an add-on. Requirements become responsive, polished interfaces,
refined with the client until the flow feels right.

# AI-integrated solutions

Chat and voice assistants, semantic search and matching, automated summarization, and photo or image
analysis woven into real workflows.

# Custom platforms

Bespoke systems that pull a scattered, manual process into one product, with booking, payments,
notifications and access in a single place.

# Automation and integrations

Workflow automation with n8n, plus integrations for Wix, payment gateways (Rivhit, RevenueCat),
WhatsApp, notifications and IoT devices.

# Maintenance and support

Long-term care covering performance, upgrades and adapting the product as the audience grows,
delivered across time zones.
