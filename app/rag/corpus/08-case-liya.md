# Liya AI - a wellbeing platform that listens (2026)

Liya AI is an intelligent employee-wellbeing platform for stress management. It blends conversational
AI, real-time surveys and analytics with access to real human therapists - automation where it helps,
people where it matters.

# The problem

Organizations could not see stress and burnout building until it was too late. Traditional surveys
arrived too slowly to act on, employees held back for fear of being identified, and HR teams had no
realistic way to read and act on large volumes of open feedback.

# What CloseFuture built

- Liya Coach: a conversational AI assistant supporting both text and voice, so employees can raise
  concerns in whatever way feels safe.
- Survey management: tools to create and send surveys capturing mood and engagement in real time.
- Voice-enabled interaction: natural-language input for intuitive, low-friction use.
- Automated summarization: AI turning raw survey data into clear, actionable insight.
- Analytics dashboard: interactive visual reporting with advanced filters for spotting trends across
  teams and segments.

The platform ships as both web and mobile applications and was completed in 2026.

# Outcome

Better visibility into wellbeing and faster, data-driven decisions. Voice and chat pushed participation
up, while therapist integration kept the support genuinely personal.
