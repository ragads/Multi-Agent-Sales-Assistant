# The toolkit

- Low-code app builders: Bubble, FlutterFlow
- Web, design and sites: Framer
- AI development: Claude Code, Lovable, Replit
- Backend and database: Supabase
- Automation and workflows: n8n
- Payments: Rivhit, RevenueCat
- Integrations: Wix API, WhatsApp, push and email notifications, IoT device control

CloseFuture runs a dedicated practice page for each core platform - Bubble, Framer, Supabase,
FlutterFlow, n8n, Lovable and Replit - a sign of how deep the specialization goes rather than a
generalist dabbling in everything.
