# Dipy - a marketplace for user-generated content (Israel, 2026)

Dipy is an Israeli marketplace that connects brand owners with content creators. Brands post orders for
videos, photos or graphics; creators browse, apply, produce the work and get paid.

# The problem

Existing UGC solutions were fragmented. A brand wanting content had to stitch together separate tools
for discovering creators, running quality assurance, managing revisions, handling payments and staying
compliant on the legal side. Creators had no single place to find good opportunities or track earnings.

# What CloseFuture built

A production-grade platform on Bubble, split into four purpose-built surfaces:

- Brand-owner desktop: a command hub for creating orders, searching the creator database, sending
  direct invitations and tracking everything in flight.
- Content-creator mobile: a native-feeling interface tuned to a 420px width for browsing orders,
  applying, uploading work, handling revisions and tracking earnings.
- Brand-owner mobile: a touch-optimized version for managing orders and talking to creators on the go.
- Admin panel: creator verification, order oversight and payment processing for the operations team.

# The details that mattered

AI semantic search matching creators to orders by language, interests and personal characteristics.
Full bilingual support in Hebrew and English. Automated payments handling VAT, discount codes and
partial credits. Notifications across four channels: push, in-app messaging, WhatsApp and email. A
creator level-progression system unlocking higher-value opportunities. Direct creator invitations
running alongside organic applications.

# Outcome

Dipy launched as a production marketplace handling hundreds of concurrent orders, with approvals and
payments running largely on their own.
