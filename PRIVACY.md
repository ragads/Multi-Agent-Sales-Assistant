# Privacy Policy - CloseFuture Assistant

_Last updated: 3 October 2026_

CloseFuture Assistant is the chat assistant on the CloseFuture website. It answers questions about
CloseFuture and books 30-minute discovery calls. This page explains what it does with data.

## What the assistant collects

- **Chat messages** you send, and the assistant's replies.
- **Details you choose to share**: name, email address, company, project description, budget or
  timeline.
- **Your browser's time zone**, so meeting times are shown in your local time.
- A **random visitor ID** stored in your browser, so your conversation continues after a page reload.

## Google Calendar access

The assistant uses the Google Calendar API, signed in as the calendar owner (CloseFuture), to:

- check when the owner's calendar is free, so it can offer you open times;
- create, move or cancel the discovery call you book, with a Google Meet link, and send you the
  calendar invitation.

It only accesses the CloseFuture owner's calendar. It never reads, changes or stores the calendars
of website visitors. Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide this
scheduling feature, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties
except as needed to provide the service.

## How data is used

- To answer your questions and book the call you ask for.
- To send a summary of the conversation to CloseFuture's sales inbox, so the founder can follow up.

## Where data is stored

- Conversations and booking details are stored in CloseFuture's database (Supabase), with access
  restricted so each visitor's session is isolated from others.
- Replies are generated by an AI model provider, which receives the messages needed to write each
  reply.
- Inactive conversations are closed after 30 days.
